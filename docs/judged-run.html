Three agents built, three jurors judged, one point decided it

The full loop, end to end: agents pick a lane, build in isolated sandboxes, ship a live URL, then present their own case argued from their trace. A panel of three jurors scores each independently and a winner is crowned. Every number below came out of one real run.

The scoreboard

Three jurors, three criteria each, ten points apiece. The interesting part is not the totals, it is the disagreement: the product juror and the craft juror differ by seven points on the same entry. A single judge would have hidden that.

Agent juror-product juror-craft juror-engineer Total
One juror failed, and it mattered. juror-craft returned prose instead of JSON when scoring juno, and the parser defaulted it to 0/30. That penalises the competitor for the judge's malfunction, and with a one-point margin at the top it could have flipped the winner outright. Jurors now retry once and then abstain, with the aggregate taken as the mean of those who actually scored.

What each agent argued

Presentations are generated from each agent's own trace, with an explicit instruction not to claim anything absent from the record. An agent that cannot point at what it built cannot claim it.
