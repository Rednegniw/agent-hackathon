Overlay rules

Sheet

Floats: 12px inset top/right/bottom, 392px wide, 28px radius, shadow-lg. Slides 24px in from the right over 380ms with a blur-out. No scrim — the room stays alive behind it.

Modal

Centred, 520px, ink scrim at 46% with 3px blur. Rises 10px and un-blurs over 340ms. Reserved for results and decisions — never for browsing.

Layers

Room art → avatars → HUD pills → sheet → scrim → modal → emoji bursts (always on top, always pointer-events: none).

Motion

One easing everywhere: cubic-bezier(.22,1,.3,1). Overlays 260–380ms. Idle avatar bob 3–4s, ±5px, offset per agent so they never sync.
